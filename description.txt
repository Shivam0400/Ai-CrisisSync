CrisisSync India is a high-fidelity emergency management and synchronization platform designed to eliminate delays in critical response. Developed as part of the Google Solution Challenge 2026, our solution addresses fragmented communication in high-density environments like hospitality hubs and urban centers.

Our platform provides a "Single Pane of Glass" for dispatchers, featuring:
1.  AI Severity Classification: Automatically categorizing distress reports to prioritize life-threatening situations.
2.  Digital Twin Grid Mapping: A real-time visual dashboard for precise incident location tracking within complex layouts.
3.  Autonomous Synchronization: Proximity-based algorithms that coordinate the most efficient routes and units for first responders.

Current Prototype Status:
For the initial prototype, we have focused our implementation on Bhagalpur, Bihar. This localized approach allows us to validate our grid-mapping telemetry and response algorithms in a real-world urban environment. 

Future Vision:
While currently operating in Bhagalpur, CrisisSync India is architected for national scale. Our roadmap involves expanding the platform across all of India to create a unified, AI-driven emergency infrastructure that saves lives through intelligent synchronization.
